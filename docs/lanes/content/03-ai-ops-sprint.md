# AI Ops Sprint, two weeks

**$2,500**

A two-week engagement focused on an agreed operations scope. Start by defining the work and what completion means—not by assuming every operational problem can fit into a sprint.

**Primary action: Request a free consultation** → `/#consultation`

## What you walk away with

The work and deliverables agreed for the two-week sprint. Before you commit, we define the operational problem, the boundaries of the engagement, and the criteria for accepting the result.

## What's included

- A two-week sprint.
- A scope agreed before the engagement, with the deliverables and acceptance criteria stated explicitly.
- For any software build included in that agreed scope: documented scripts, a Dockerized build for portability, and README, quickstart, CurrentState, manifest, index, handoff, and troubleshooting documentation.
- For that build work: stack-specific workflows and skills, file-level provenance, tests run green before handoff, page contracts where pages are involved, and API and Digital Intelligence (DI) flow documentation where applicable. A Digital Intelligence (DI) integration includes its maintenance collaborator alongside the system component.

## What's not included

The two-week window is not an open-ended operations engagement. Unspecified implementation, integrations, follow-on changes, and ongoing maintenance are not promised by the offer name or price. Any work outside the agreed scope needs a separate agreement.

Digital Intelligence (DI) is not added simply to fill a package. It has to fit the problem.

## Establish fit before defining the sprint

**Free consultation — $0:** explain the operations problem; we establish fit. The call does not include free specification work or free design.

**Discovery (scoped, paid) — $600:** a separate planning engagement gathers goals, constraints, and specifications and presents the route forward. Credited toward build on acceptance. Whether discovery is needed for your sprint is made clear before you proceed.
