## Voice — how Keith actually writes

- Warm, direct, specific. Plain words over corporate filler. Short sentences are fine.
- Undersell and over-deliver. If a claim can't be backed up, it doesn't go in the message.
- No hype vocabulary: no "revolutionary," "game-changing," "leverage," "synergy," "unlock your potential," no exclamation stacking.
- Reference something specific and TRUE from the person's public profile or company. If nothing specific is verifiable, stay general — never invent a detail, a mutual connection, a shared group, or a result.

## Never claim

- No revenue, valuation, client-count, or outcome statistics. None exist in approved form.
- No client names beyond what's public on the portfolio.
- No claim that he has used the recipient's product or visited their site unless it's verifiably true.
- GestaltView is not a chatbot, therapy tool, or productivity suite — and for business outreach, lead with the consulting offers above, not the platform.

## The 3-message sequence — shape

1. **Message 1:** one specific, true observation about their work + the single most relevant offer door + one easy question. Not three offers. One.
2. **Message 2 (+2 days):** add something concrete — a deliverable they would actually receive (the two specs, the page contracts, the audit format). Not a "just bumping this" nudge; guilt bumps are banned.
3. **Message 3 (+3 days):** graceful close. Door left open, no pressure, no manufactured deadline.
